ANALYSIS
========

WHAT'S WORKING:
- Heroes now target and reach enemy-occupied nodes successfully
- Average 5.2 enemies defeated per game is strong (likely 50-60% of spawned enemies)
- 19 zone boss kills across 500 games (3.8%) shows heroes CAN beat bosses
- Resource gathering operational (avg 2.6/game)
- Colony card economy functional (avg 3.9 colony cards played)

WHAT'S NOT WORKING:
1. Heroes still die by turn 3-4 (avg 3.9 turns, down from 4.6 in v2/v3)
   - Hypothesis: heroes now rush INTO combat and die there, whereas before
     they wandered nearby nodes and died to starvation instead
   - Trading starvation death for combat death — still dying too fast

2. 0% win rate — Pied Piper is never reached
   - Pied Piper is at the far end of the map (node ~30)
   - Heroes need 8-10+ turns to reach it
   - But average game lasts 3.9 turns

3. Resources gathered dropped (2.6 vs 3.6 in v2/v3)
   - Heroes gather on the way to combat but die in combat
   - No heroes survive to return resources to colony

4. Food economy still unsustainable
   - Base 2 food feeds 2 heroes
   - Colony cards ramp +1/turn
   - But heroes die in combat before food economy matures

REMAINING BALANCE ISSUES
=========================

PRIMARY: Heroes die too fast (turns 3-4 average game length)
  Root causes:
  a) Starvation weakens heroes before combat (1 HP/turn for unfed heroes)
  b) Combat damage kills weakened heroes easily
  c) All heroes rush to fight — no staggered deployment
  d) No healing mechanic — damage is permanent

RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. INCREASE BASE FOOD: 2 → 6 (feed more heroes without starvation)
2. INCREASE HERO HP: Scale all heroes +2-3 HP (more combat rounds survived)
3. STAGGER DEPLOYMENT: Deploy scouts first, fighters later when food is ready
4. HEALING AT COLONY: Heroes at colony node should regain 1-2 HP per turn
5. RETREAT BEHAVIOR: Heroes below 50% HP should return to colony
6. TACTICAL CARDS: Auto-play should use tactical cards in combat for burst damage
