ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
===================

1. COMBAT IS NEVER WON (Critical)
   0 enemies defeated in 500 games means heroes never win a single fight.
   Possible causes:
   a) Heroes never reach enemy nodes (movement/pathing issue)
   b) Combat is too lopsided (enemy strength >> hero strength)
   c) Heroes die to starvation before combat occurs
   d) Combat results aren't being tracked correctly

2. FOOD RAMP IS TOO SLOW
   With 1 colony card per turn and food cards producing +1 each,
   it takes 6 turns to reach self-sufficiency. Meanwhile, heroes
   deployed in turns 1-3 are starving.

3. HERO DEPLOYMENT IS STILL SUBOPTIMAL
   Deploying heroes before food economy is ready means they starve.
   But NOT deploying means no map exploration and no progress.
   This is a fundamental design tension.

BALANCE RECOMMENDATIONS
========================

IMMEDIATE FIXES (No Design Changes Required):
1. Increase base food production: 2 → 6
   - Allows 6 heroes to be fed from turn 1
   - Colony cards then push production to 10+ for full deployment

2. Increase food production per card: +1 → +2 or +3
   - Makes each colony card more impactful
   - Faster ramp to self-sufficiency

3. Remove or reduce starvation damage for first 3 turns
   - Grace period while colony is being established
   - Or: heroes carry 1 turn of rations when deployed

DESIGN-LEVEL FIXES (May Require GDD Changes):
4. Make colony cards playable from hand during Deploy phase
   - Currently limited to 1 per Colony phase
   - Allow 2-3 colony cards per turn for faster ramp

5. Heroes should return to colony when low HP
   - Auto-play (and real players) need a heal/retreat mechanic
   - Currently heroes march forward until they die

6. Consider removing the food-per-hero model entirely
   - Replace with flat colony upkeep (e.g., 5 food/turn regardless)
   - Focus food on healing and buffs instead

7. Equipment should provide more survivability
   - Current equipment barely helps against starvation
   - Consider: equipment that reduces food requirement
   - Or: equipment that provides passive healing

INVESTIGATION NEEDED:
- Why are 0 enemies being defeated? Need combat logs.
- Are heroes reaching enemy-occupied nodes at all?
- Is the combat resolver working correctly?
- Are enemy positions and counts reasonable?
