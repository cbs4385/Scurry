ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
===================

PRIMARY ISSUE: STARVATION (Critical)
  - 18 deployed heroes need 18 food/turn
  - Colony produces only 2 food/turn (base rate)
  - 16 heroes starve EVERY turn, losing 1 HP each
  - Low-HP heroes (Messenger 2HP, Assassin 2HP, Rogue 2HP) are at 1 HP
    before their first combat — they die to ANY damage
  - Even tanky heroes (Bulwark 6HP, Guardian 5HP) lose 1 HP/turn and
    crumble after 3-4 turns of starvation

  Impact: Starvation deals 16 damage to the party every turn.
          Over 5 turns, that's 80 total HP drained before combat.
          The entire hero pool has ~60 total HP — starvation alone
          kills the party by turn 4-5 even without combat.

SECONDARY ISSUE: COMBAT DAMAGE DISTRIBUTION
  - Enemy damage is applied to lowest-combat heroes first (per GDD)
  - But starvation already reduced those heroes to 1 HP
  - A hero at 1 HP dies to any combat damage, even 1 point
  - This means every combat kills multiple heroes regardless of strength ratio

  Example: Combat #1, heroes dealt 19 vs enemies' 19 (even match)
           Heroes killed 2 of 3 enemies BUT all 6 heroes died
           Why: Heroes had 1-5 HP (avg ~2.5) vs 19 total enemy damage
                19 damage ÷ ~2.5 avg HP = all 6 heroes killed

TERTIARY ISSUE: RESOURCE LOOP NEVER STARTS
  - Heroes gather resources on map nodes during Gather phase
  - But heroes must RETURN to colony to deposit resources
  - Heroes getting injured drop their carried resources
  - With starvation killing heroes before they can return,
    the colony never receives food → starvation continues → death spiral

BALANCE RECOMMENDATIONS
========================

1. REDUCE FOOD REQUIREMENT
   - Current: 1 food per deployed hero per turn (18 food for 18 heroes)
   - Option A: 1 food per 3 heroes (6 food for 18 heroes)
   - Option B: Only deployed-away-from-colony heroes need food
   - Option C: Flat food cost (e.g., 5/turn regardless of hero count)

2. INCREASE BASE FOOD PRODUCTION
   - Current: 2 food/turn from basic colony
   - Suggested: 8-10 food/turn base, upgradeable via colony cards
   - This allows 8-10 heroes to be fed without colony investment

3. STARVATION SHOULD NOT KILL
   - Instead of 1 HP damage, starving heroes could:
     - Lose 1 combat strength (weakened, not dying)
     - Be unable to move (grounded until fed)
     - Take 1 damage every OTHER turn (slower death spiral)

4. HERO HP IS TOO LOW FOR POOLED COMBAT
   - With pooled damage, low-HP heroes die instantly
   - Consider: minimum 3 HP for all heroes
   - Or: damage is split evenly across heroes (not lowest-first)
   - Or: heroes can only take damage up to their combat value (tougher fighters absorb more)

5. VICTORY CONDITION IS UNREACHABLE
   - Pied Piper is at the far end of the map
   - Heroes need 6+ turns just to traverse there
   - But the colony dies by turn 4-5
   - Even with perfect balance, the food economy must sustain 10+ turns
