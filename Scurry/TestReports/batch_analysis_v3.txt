ROOT CAUSE: FUNDAMENTAL BALANCE ISSUE
=======================================

The code fixes (retargeting, enemy tracking) are working correctly — verified
in single-game logged runs where 2 enemies were defeated. However, in the
standard game flow, heroes NEVER reach enemy-occupied nodes because:

1. FOOD BOTTLENECK (Primary)
   - Base food production: 2/turn (from Basic Burrow)
   - Only 2 heroes can deploy turn 1 (food-limited)
   - Each colony card adds +1 food → +1 hero per subsequent turn
   - By turn 5: 6 food, 6 heroes deployed... but heroes deployed on turns 1-2
     have been starving for 3-4 turns and are at 1 HP or dead

2. STARVATION DEATH SPIRAL
   - Heroes deployed turn 1: 2 food, 2 heroes = OK
   - Turn 2: 3 food, 3 heroes → 0 unfed (barely balanced)
   - Turn 3: 4 food, 4 heroes → 0 unfed (still balanced IF food ramps)
   - BUT: Food production only ramps if colony cards are DRAWN AND PLAYED
   - Colony phase draws 1 card/turn from colony deck → random chance of food card
   - If non-food colony card is drawn (FogReveal, CombatBuff), heroes starve

3. HERO REACH DEFICIT
   - Heroes have move 2-4 per turn
   - First Wilderness enemies are 2-3 hops from colony
   - A hero with move 2 needs 2 turns to reach first enemy
   - But heroes at 2-3 HP die to 1 turn of starvation + any combat damage
   - By the time heroes reach enemies, they're too weak to win

4. DEPLOYMENT TIMING PARADOX
   - Early deployment → heroes starve (not enough food)
   - Late deployment → heroes never reach enemies (game too short)
   - Optimal: deploy 2 scouts turn 1, add 1/turn → but 15-turn game means
     heroes need to be surviving AND progressing, not just surviving

BALANCE RECOMMENDATIONS (PRIORITY ORDER)
==========================================

1. INCREASE BASE FOOD PRODUCTION: 2 → 6
   Why: Allows 6 heroes to deploy turn 1 without starvation
   Impact: Heroes reach first enemies by turn 2, creating actual combat encounters
   Risk: May make food-producing colony cards less valuable

2. REDUCE STARVATION DAMAGE TO 0 FOR FIRST 3 TURNS
   Why: Grace period while colony ramps up (like real ant colonies)
   Impact: Early heroes survive to reach enemies
   Alternative: Give each hero 2 "rations" when deployed

3. INCREASE HERO BASE HP: minimum 3 HP for all heroes
   Why: 2 HP heroes (Messenger, Assassin, Rogue) die to 2 turns of starvation
   Impact: All heroes can survive at least 3 turns of starvation
   Note: Already would be fixed by recommendation #1

4. ALLOW MULTIPLE COLONY CARDS PER TURN (2-3)
   Why: 1 colony card/turn means food ramps too slowly
   Impact: Colony can reach self-sufficiency by turn 3 instead of turn 6

5. CONSIDER FLAT FOOD COST (5/turn regardless of hero count)
   Why: Eliminates the N*food scaling that makes large parties impossible
   Impact: Colony management becomes about upgrades, not survival math
   Risk: Changes strategic depth — may be too much of a design shift
